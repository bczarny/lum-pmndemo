ADELA's PRIMA Debuts at No. 1 Overall as Phoebe Bridgers Reclaims Indie Record Stores

CHRIS MURATORE, DIRECTOR, PARTNERSHIPS    SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 — 4 MIN READ

ADELA's highly anticipated PRIMA claims the top spot on both the physical and vinyl charts this week, while Stray Kids ensures K-Pop continues its powerful dominance of brick-and-mortar storefronts.

The story this week

  • Overall market surges +17.8% YoY: Driven by major new releases and strong retail activity, overall physical album sales crossed 1.5 million units (1,503,959), rebounding sharply compared to 1.28 million units in the same week of 2025.
  • ADELA debuts at No. 1 overall: ADELA’s PRIMA captured the #1 spot on both the overall physical chart (27,894 sales) and the overall vinyl chart (11,984 sales), with physical formats driving 50% of the album's total first-week consumption units.
  • Vinyl leads week-over-week format expansion: Vinyl albums experienced a massive +28.0% YoY increase to 850,611 units, supported by double-digit gains across D2C (+40%), mass market (+30%), independent stores (+28%), and e-commerce (+22%).
  • Phoebe Bridgers reclaims indie crown: Phoebe Bridgers’ Lost Weekend returned to #1 at independent record stores, leading the indie physical chart with 3,008 sales and the indie vinyl chart with 2,053 sales.
  • Independent record stores up +33%: Indie retailers accounted for 34% of all physical sales nationwide (512,402 units), marking the seventh consecutive week of positive YoY raw same-store growth (+38%).
  • K-Pop maintains physical storefront reliance: Stray Kids’ THIS & THAT held the #1 overall CD slot with 25,400 CD sales, with 23,501 copies (92.5%) purchased directly in physical storefront locations.

Indie store momentum continues

Independent record stores moved 512,402 physical albums this week, up 33% from 385,327 in the same week last year. Vinyl represented a huge chunk of that volume with 338,787 units (+28% YoY), while indie CD album sales reached 168,000 (+26% YoY).

Physical sales landscape

Year-to-date physical album sales stand at 53.1 million units (+6.4% YoY). Vinyl continues to lead overall volume at 29.8 million units (+2.7%), while CDs maintain double-digit growth (+11.6%) reaching 23.0 million units.

Factoids

  • 50%: Share of ADELA’s debut consumption units for PRIMA generated purely by physical sales.
  • 23,501: In-store CD copies sold of Stray Kids’ THIS & THAT, demonstrating heavy brick-and-mortar storefront reliance.
  • 10,542: Vinyl copies moved by Beyoncé’s 2006 album B'Day, propelling it to the #2 spot on the overall vinyl chart.
  • 4.8M: Total physical albums sold at independent record stores through the third quarter of 2026 to date.
